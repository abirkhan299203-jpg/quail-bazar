# quail-bazar
Quail Bazar - Bangladesh Quail Marketplace
## About Quail Bazar

Quail Bazar is a marketplace connecting quail farmers, buyers, and sellers across Bangladesh.

## Main Categories
- Live Quail Birds
- Quail Chicks
- Quail Eggs
- Quail Feed
- Farm Equipment and Supplies

## Features
- Bengali and English language support
- Post and manage advertisements
- Search by district and upazila
- Direct contact by phone and WhatsApp
- Product photos, prices, and descriptions
- Free advertisements at launch

## Important
Quail Bazar connects buyers and sellers. The platform does not directly buy or sell products.
